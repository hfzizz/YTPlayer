YT Mini Player

Controls the YouTube tab that played most recently. The video keeps playing in that tab; the mini player is a remote.

Player modes

Toolbar button

Overlay

Queue

Keyboard shortcuts

    Change them in about:addons → gear icon → Manage Extension Shortcuts.

    Inside the popup, sidebar and mini window: Space/K play/pause, N next, P previous, M mute, C captions, ←/→ seek 5s.
