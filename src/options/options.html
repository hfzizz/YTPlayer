YT Mini Player

Search YouTube, build a queue and control playback from anywhere. The audio plays in a YouTube tab (opened for you when needed); this is the remote. In Zen Browser, Zen's own media player and your media keys skip through your queue too.

Keyboard shortcuts

    Change them in about:addons → gear icon → Manage Extension Shortcuts.

    Inside the popup, sidebar and mini window: Space/K play/pause, N next, P previous, M mute, C captions, ←/→ seek 5s, / search, Q queue.
